How it works

  1. Open any site and play the video, song or open the photo.
  2. The ⬇ download button lights up with the number of files found.
  3. Tap it, pick the quality, and it saves to Download/Grabbit.

Note

  1. Sites that stream with DRM or in protected chunks may show no downloadable file.
  2. Only save content you have the right to download.
