Grabbit 1.2.0
- Built-in video and audio player (ExoPlayer): plays saved files and streams found on a page before you download them.
- Gestures: tap for controls, double-tap left/right to seek 10 s, swipe left edge for brightness, right edge for volume.
- Speed control, quality picker for HLS streams, subtitles, rotate, picture-in-picture.
- Audio keeps playing in the background with notification and lock-screen controls.
- Grabbit now shows up as a player when you open video or audio files from other apps.
