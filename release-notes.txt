Grabbit 1.1.0
- Share a link from YouTube, Instagram or any app to Grabbit: the page opens and the download sheet pops up by itself.
- Copied a link? Open Grabbit and a "Grab it" banner offers to fetch it.
- Media sheet grouped into Videos / Audio / Images with thumbnails, duration badges and file sizes.
- Images keep their real file names; tracking pixels and HTML pages are filtered out.
- Downloads list shows thumbnails of saved files.
