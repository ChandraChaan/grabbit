Grabbit 1.5.2
- Instagram fixed (verified on a public reel): the video URLs live inside the page's JSON, not in network requests, and Instagram escapes them in a way that cut off the signature. Grabbit now reads inline and GraphQL JSON correctly, offers the full-quality MP4 with sound, and pairs separate video/audio tracks for merging.
- Requests made through a site's service worker are now sniffed too (Instagram, Facebook and other PWAs).
- Media URLs found in XHR/fetch responses are listed as well, which helps on X, TikTok and Facebook.
