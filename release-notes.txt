Grabbit 1.4.0
- YouTube fixed: links are resolved with a real extractor. Download now offers every quality (144p to 2160p) and audio-only; video and audio are merged on the phone into one MP4.
- Quality picker: tapping Download on anything with multiple qualities (YouTube, HLS streams) lets you choose resolution, format and size.
- HLS streams are saved as standard MP4 files instead of .ts.
- Sites that offer in-page (blob:) downloads now work.
- Instagram/Facebook byte-range chunk URLs are collapsed into full tracks.
- Download failures now show a reason in the list instead of silently saving junk.
